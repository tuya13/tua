How did i get into junk journaling?

It all actually started with my love for stationery products, which dates back to my childhood. I had a sticker collection when I was around eleven. I’ve always loved colored pencils, notebooks, cute pens, and watching videos about them.

After a while, I started seeing content on YouTube related to journaling, junk journaling, scrapbooking, and bullet journaling. That’s how I entered this world. The opportunity to be creative brought me a sense of peace. I began trying to recreate these styles in my own notebooks, and over time—as I started having my own money—I was able to use a wider variety of materials.

I still pursue this hobby today, and I hope to continue doing so forever. It has always helped me clear my mind and brought me peace; it also gives me an aesthetic perspective. That’s why I love journaling.
